# OSU Purchasing Card Policy (condensed for transaction review)

Condensed from *OSU Purchasing Card Guidelines*, May 2026 (`source/Pcard-Guidelines-2026.05.pdf`). Covers department Pcards only. Card security, Works roles, record retention and student-organization cards are left out. Each rule carries the ID used in `osu_rules.yaml`. Sections 1-5 give the whole policy for context; section 6 lists the checks you decide. Cite the check ID (e.g. `OSU-PRH-10b`) for those, or the rule ID when pointing to other policy. If this summary and the PDF disagree, the PDF wins.

**How to read a rule:** **Prohibited** means never allowed on the Pcard. **Conditional** means allowed only if the stated condition is met. **Docs** means the required documents or approvals must exist. **Guidance** is a "should". A purchase that is not discovered is not thereby approved.

## 1. Limits and splitting

- **OSU-LIM-01**: A single transaction may not exceed **$10,000**.
- **OSU-LIM-02**: Total spend per card per billing cycle (21st to 20th) may not exceed **$50,000**. A monthly limit over $20,000 needs department justification.
- **OSU-LIM-03**: Student-employee cards need justification for a single-transaction limit over $500 or a monthly limit over $2,500.
- **OSU-SPL-01**: **Split purchases are prohibited.** Dividing or failing to consolidate a known need to stay under the **$10,000** bid threshold, whether across swipes, cardholders, projects, cycles or months, vendors, or funds.
- **OSU-EQP-01** (Docs): For equipment of **$5,000 or more**, the invoice must go to Asset Management within 24 hours.

## 2. Applies to every purchase

- **OSU-GEN-04** (Docs): An itemized receipt is required: date, merchant, itemized items and prices, total including shipping.
- **OSU-GEN-05** (Docs): A description and the business purpose must be entered in Works.
- **OSU-GEN-03** (Docs): Deliver to a university address, unless an approved exception form is kept with the receipt.
- **OSU-GEN-01** (Guidance): Check OK Corral first. Use system-wide contracts when they exist.
- **OSU-GEN-02** (Prohibited): No purchases from OSU departments or businesses (University Dining, University Mailing, OSU Ticket Office, etc.). Use the campus vendor invoice (CVI) system.
- **OSU-GEN-06** (Prohibited): Department cards may not buy for student organizations.
- **OSU-GEN-07** (Prohibited): Department cards may not make ledger-9 purchases.
- **OSU-GEN-08** (Prohibited): No purchases from friends or family, companies owned by a university employee, or companies where the cardholder has a financial interest.
- **OSU-GEN-09** (Prohibited): Only the named cardholder may use the card or its number. No sharing or loaning.
- **OSU-GEN-10** (Conditional): Don't pay a restocking fee when the merchant caused the problem. Get a credit receipt and confirm the credit posts.
- **OSU-PRH-17** (Prohibited): No **Oklahoma sales tax** on any purchase.
- **OSU-RST-04** (Conditional): Card processing / convenience fees are capped at **4%**.
- **OSU-RST-10** (Conditional): Items for resale only by areas holding a sales tax permit.

## 4. Allowed with conditions

- **OSU-RST-05**: **Food and drink** must meet the AVP food guidelines. Otherwise a VP or Dean approval letter is needed *before* purchase, with no delegation. Professional development retreat food needs prior Dean/VP approval. Student retreat food is allowed on 9 funds; other funds need Dean/VP approval.
- **OSU-RST-06**: **Gift cards for human subjects** only with OSU Controller preapproval, at most **$100 per card**.
- **OSU-RST-07**: **Fuel** is allowed for department equipment, gas cans and eligible rental cars. It is not allowed for personal or university vehicles.
- **OSU-RST-08**: **PPE** (lab coats, gloves, boots, ice cleats) *may* be allowed with department/college approval and stays university property.
- **OSU-RST-03**: **Student clothing** is allowed on a ledger-9 fund. Otherwise, for students representing OSU at official events, only at **$25 or less per item**, including embellishments.
- **OSU-RST-09**: **Registration fees** may be paid at or after the event. They may be prepaid only if documented as either (1) early discount + substitute allowed + full refund if canceled, or (2) prepayment required + purchase orders not accepted.
- **OSU-RST-02**: **Licenses and certifications**: if required before hiring, the employee pays. If required later, OSU may pay the initial fee only; the employee pays renewals.
- **OSU-RST-11**: **Non-licensed service providers** (trainers, speakers) are allowed. Their travel must be on their invoice. A plaque or certificate is allowed only if unpaid. No tokens of appreciation for OSU employees.
- **OSU-RST-13**: **Space heaters** need written EHS preapproval attached to the receipt.
- **OSU-RST-14**: **Sponsorships** only if OSU receives something in return, documented.
- **OSU-RST-15**: **Student awards**: non-monetary awards (trophies, plaques, certificates) are allowed. Cash and gifts are prohibited elsewhere (PRH-02, PRH-10).
- **OSU-RST-16**: **Vehicle tags**: the initial registration goes through Asset Management. Single-year renewals are allowed.
- **OSU-RST-17**: **Tips** are capped at **20%**, paid together with the service.
- **OSU-RST-18**: **Data plans** only for university-owned devices.
- **OSU-RST-01** (Guidance): Buy Apple products through the University Store (Stillwater) when available.
- **OSU-RST-12** (Guidance): Check central mailing before paying for outbound shipping.

## 5. Travel

**Before travel**
- **OSU-TRV-01** (Docs): Airfare, out-of-state lodging and out-of-state ground transport need an Out-of-State/Country Travel Request, fully approved *before* purchase (two approvals above the traveler; international also needs the Dean of OSU Global).
- **OSU-TRV-02** (Docs): The trip number (T#) must be entered in Works for airfare, lodging, rideshare and car rental.
- **OSU-TRV-15** (Prohibited): If personal activities are planned with the trip, the Pcard may not pay related travel.
- **OSU-TRV-16** (Prohibited): No Pcard travel for anyone receiving an honorarium or other compensation.

**Airfare**
- **OSU-TRV-03** (Docs): Book through **Anthony Travel** (the contracted agency). Otherwise attach an Anthony Travel quote showing the purchase was cheaper.
- **OSU-TRV-04** (Prohibited): **Economy class only.** No Early Bird check-in, seat upgrades or similar unless there is a medical or business reason. Normal baggage fees are allowed; overweight or extra personal bags are not.

**Lodging**
- **OSU-TRV-06** (Conditional): Room rate is capped at the **GSA rate** unless documented (event rate, event at the same hotel, or a Self-Designated Lodging Form). For Airbnb/VRBO, include cleaning and service fees.
- **OSU-TRV-05** (Conditional): Prepayment only if refundable, with the cancellation policy attached.
- **OSU-TRV-07** (Prohibited): **No taxes on Oklahoma lodging** (sales, occupancy, city, etc.). Tribal, TIF/TID and PACE fees are allowed.
- **OSU-TRV-08** (Prohibited): No personal charges on the folio (food, drinks, laundry, safe, pet fees).
- **OSU-TRV-09** (Prohibited): Registration and lodging may not be paid as one combined charge.
- **OSU-TRV-10** (Conditional): No-show and late-cancellation fees caused by personal reasons or negligence must be reimbursed.
- **OSU-TRV-11** (Docs): Rentals or leases over **5 nights** need Central Procurement preapproval.

**Ground transportation and parking**
- **OSU-TRV-12** (Conditional): Rideshare and taxi are for business only. No prepayment and no priority or luxury upgrades. A receipt is required for any amount.
- **OSU-TRV-13** (Conditional): **No car rental for in-state travel.** Written justification is required. For out-of-state driving, an airfare quote showing the rental is cheaper is required. No prepayment. Any personal miles mean the rental should not be on the Pcard. The final receipt must show locations and miles.
- **OSU-TRV-17** (Conditional): Business parking is allowed with a receipt. **Not on any OSU campus.** No parking or traffic tickets.
- **OSU-TRV-18** (Conditional): Passports only for university business abroad, documented.

**Meals while traveling**
- **OSU-TRV-14** (Prohibited): **Food, drink and mileage while in travel status may not go on the Pcard.** The traveler pays and claims reimbursement.

## 6. What you decide

Most rules are checked by code before a transaction reaches you: limits, MCC and merchant lists, receipt lines (tax, tips, fees, fare class), and whether required documents exist. You decide only the checks below, which ask what a purchase is, who it was for, or why.

For each check you are given, answer **violation**, **allowed** or **unclear**. Quote the evidence (receipt line, item text, stated purpose) that supports the answer. Answer **unclear** when the evidence does not show what was bought or why; do not guess. A check marked *flag* is guidance: flag it, never call it a violation.

## 8. Policy analysis

Observations on how the policy is built, where it is vague or in tension with itself, and how OSU's FY2024–25 card data lines up with it. Data figures come from the public transaction data (223,454 OSU rows, $66.5M). They are **signals, not confirmed violations**: the public data has no receipts, approvals or card limits, and it predates this May 2026 version of the guidelines.

### 8.1 What the policy is mostly about

- **It is built for review at reconciliation, not blocking at the register.** Of 74 rules, 38 are prohibitions, but 18 checks are about whether a document or approval exists and 19 checks can only be judged after a deadline (mostly cycle sign-off). Every transaction gets three sign-offs (cardholder, approver, accountant). MCC blocking exists (p. 9), but the blocked MCC list isn't published.
- **Travel is the densest area and also the biggest spend.** 18 travel rules, plus travel exceptions inside other rules (insurance, fuel, tips). Travel MCCs (airlines, lodging, agencies, rentals, taxis, parking) are roughly a quarter of OSU card spend (about 21% of rows, 24% of dollars).
- **Food rules depend on a document we don't have.** Restaurants and catering are about 5% of OSU rows and groceries another 7%, so about 12% of transactions fall under the food guidelines (OSU-RST-05), whose actual criteria live in a separate PDF.

### 8.2 Thresholds and how they interact

| Amount | Rule |
|---|---|
| $10,000 | Single-transaction limit (OSU-LIM-01) **and** the bid threshold that defines split purchases (OSU-SPL-01) |
| $50,000 / $20,000 | Monthly card limit / justification needed above $20,000 (OSU-LIM-02) |
| $5,000 | Equipment must be reported to Asset Management within 24 hours (OSU-EQP-01) |
| $500 / $2,500 | Student-employee single / monthly limits needing justification (OSU-LIM-03) |
| $100, $25 | Per human-subject gift card (OSU-RST-06); per student clothing item (OSU-RST-03) |
| 4%, 20%, 5 nights | Card fees (OSU-RST-04), tips (OSU-RST-17), leased lodging (OSU-TRV-11) |

- **The limit and the bid threshold are the same number.** Splitting a purchase is the only way to exceed $10,000, so the split-purchase rule is the real protection behind the limit.
- **Charges pile up just below $5,000, consistently.** FY2024: 184 charges at $4,500–4,999 vs 39 at $5,000–5,499. FY2025: 177 vs 50. About 24–25 charges a year land at exactly $5,000. That fits people avoiding the asset-reporting step, but it also fits card limits set at $5,000. The data can't tell these apart; card limit settings would.
- **51 OSU charges exceed the $10,000 limit** (24 in FY2024, 27 in FY2025, $973k total). 76% are travel, mostly large hotel bills and agency or airline bookings that look like group travel. 31% sit on the two highest-volume travel-looking cards, and several sit on cardholder names ending in "CH" (e.g. "Yeats CH"), which may mark a special card type (unverified). The guidelines describe no exception path above $10,000. So either higher-limit travel cards exist outside this document, or the limit was different before May 2026. As written, OSU-LIM-01 would fail all 51; it should be a review flag, not a hard fail, until the exceptions are known.

### 8.3 Where the policy is vague

- **The judgment checks exist because key terms are undefined.** "Business purpose", "personal", "decorations", "luxury services", "dangerous chemicals", "sufficient justification": the policy gives examples, never definitions. These drive the 29 LLM checks. The best fix is OSU's own interpretations (FAQs, past compliance inquiries) turned into labeled examples.
- **"Should" and "must" are used loosely.** Delivery to a university address "should always" happen, yet any exception needs a signed form, which reads as a must. Three rules are genuinely advisory (OK Corral first, Apple through the University Store, check central mailing before shipping).
- **Appendix A disclaims its own authority.** It calls itself "general information only … not a comprehensive or definitive policy document … subject to change without notice." Many rules here come from Appendix A. The binding sources are OSU Policy 3-0241 and the Procurement Guidelines, neither of which has been ingested.

### 8.4 Tensions inside the policy

The pattern is a blanket prohibition in one place with exceptions somewhere else:

| Prohibition | Exception elsewhere |
|---|---|
| "Meals" as an item without business purpose (p. 25) | Food allowed under the food guidelines (p. 24) |
| Gifts "in any form" (p. 25) | Gift cards for human subjects (p. 25); non-monetary student awards (p. 28) |
| Insurance (p. 25) | Damage insurance on an eligible car rental (p. 32) |
| Deposits and prepayments (p. 24) | Prepaid registration under Option 1/2 (p. 27); refundable lodging (p. 30); subscriptions |
| Card sharing (pp. 8, 13) | None stated, yet the data shows card keys that look shared (three keys hold about 15% of OSU's FY2025 rows). These are probably department or travel cards with a custodian, a card type the guidelines never describe. |

A reviewer reading one section in isolation would get these wrong. The rule file links them with cross-references, gates and `when` conditions.

### 8.5 Rules nothing can verify automatically

No data source records friends and family (OSU-GEN-08, beyond conflict-of-interest disclosures), personal activities on a trip (OSU-TRV-15), or whether an OK Corral contract existed without the contract catalog (OSU-GEN-01). These depend on the cardholder's attestation and on audits. Automation can at best screen for them.

### 8.7 Caveats before using this policy on historical data

- **Version.** These guidelines are dated May 2026; the transactions are from July 2023 to June 2025. Rules and limits may have changed. Findings on historical data should read "would violate the current policy", or be checked against the guidelines in force at the time.
- **Card type.** Department cards and student-organization cards can't be told apart in the public data, and student-organization rules differ (gift cards up to $25 allowed, food allowed, sales tax payable). Applying department rules to a student-organization card produces false violations.
- **Card limits.** Individual card limits are set by departments and aren't in the data. Many limit findings depend on them.

### 8.8 What this means for the pipeline

1. **Treat OSU-LIM-01 as a review flag** until OSU confirms whether higher-limit cards exist; otherwise expect about 25 false hard-fails a year.
2. **Get the food guidelines.** They govern about 12% of transactions, and right now those checks can only return "unclear".
3. **Record each rule's effective date** so the engine applies the version in force on the transaction date.
4. **Collect OSU's interpretations** of the vague terms as labeled examples. They matter more to the 29 judgment checks than prompt wording does.
5. **Start with the high-precision signals.** Amazon Prime memberships, postage stamps, liquor stores and OSU-department merchants are cheap, explicit and visible today, which makes them good first test cases for the pipeline end to end.
